About

An AI engineering business, built around operations that cannot afford surprises.

SYNQEVRA Solutions develops custom AI agents, chatbots, workflow automation and monitoring software for businesses. The work is practical: connect to the data a business already produces, explain what changed, and only then decide what the software is allowed to do on its own.

Who we are

SYNQEVRA Solutions is founded and owned by Mr. Fatir Ali. He leads the technical direction and the delivery of every engagement. The business is deliberately small and engineering-led: fewer promises, written scope, and software that can be inspected.

What we do

  • AI agents that read operational data and propose actions under human approval
  • Chatbots and voice agents for service intake and after-hours coverage
  • Workflow automation between the tools a business already pays for
  • Monitoring and reporting for data centers: UPS, power, cooling, generators
  • Condition monitoring for compressors, pumps and cooling plant

How we work

Every project starts read-only. We prove the baseline before automating anything, we classify actions by risk, and we write down what the software is not allowed to do. Where a system touches safety-critical equipment, a qualified engineer and a human operator stay in the loop.

What we do not do

We do not publish client names, savings figures or accuracy claims we cannot evidence. We do not present prototypes as production deployments, and we do not sell software we do not have the rights to distribute.
