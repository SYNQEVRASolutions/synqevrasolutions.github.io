Privacy

Short version: this website has no tracking scripts, no cookies and no analytics. The enquiry form opens your own email client, so the only copy of your message is the email you send us.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. Your name, company, email and the problem you describe, sent to fatircompanyai@gmail.com. We use it to reply and, if we work together, to write a scope.
  • Nothing automatic. No cookies, no advertising pixels, no third-party analytics on this site.
  • Server logs. Our host (GitHub Pages) records standard web requests under its own policies.

What we do with it

We answer you. We do not sell, rent or share enquiry details with third parties. We do not add you to a mailing list. If we are not going to work together, we delete the correspondence on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any information you have sent by emailing fatircompanyai@gmail.com. We will confirm when it is removed.

Client work

For live projects, access to customer systems is granted read-only and scoped, credentials are stored in the customer's own secret manager wherever possible, and we do not publish customer data, names or screenshots without written permission.
