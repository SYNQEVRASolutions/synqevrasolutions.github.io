Service

AI Agent Development

Custom AI agents that read your operational data, explain what changed, and propose the next action - with a human approval gate before anything executes.

The problem

Your team already has the data. What is missing is the layer that reads it, decides what matters, and tells the right person before the problem becomes downtime.

What we build

  • Read-only baseline first: two weeks of data before any action is allowed
  • Propose, do not act: the agent drafts the action with its evidence
  • Approval gates by risk class, with hard caps per hour
  • An audit trail that answers what it knew, and when

What it looks like in practice

We start with a read-only connection to the data you already produce, run it alongside your current process, and only add automated actions once the baseline is proven and the approval gate is agreed in writing.
