Data Center AI Software: Cooling, Power and Alarms

Monitoring and reporting for data center cooling and power, designed to reduce alarm noise rather than add another screen.

The operational problem

A site can produce hundreds of alarms a day. The useful question is not how many, but which three matter now, what changed in the last hour, and who is responsible for the next action. That correlation work is still largely manual in small and mid-size facilities.

What we build

  • Rack inlet temperature and environmental sensor dashboards
  • CRAC/CRAH status visibility and cooling performance trends
  • UPS, PDU and generator alarm ingestion, read-only
  • Alarm de-duplication and correlation into a single incident
  • Shift and incident reports generated with the evidence attached
  • Energy and efficiency trend views where metering allows
  • Operator decision support with an approval gate before any action

Integration reality

We work with the data your site already produces: SNMP where the equipment exposes it, Modbus or OPC-UA where a gateway exists, vendor exports and BMS/DCIM reports where neither is available. Collection is read-only. If a protocol is not supported by your hardware, we say so before quoting rather than discovering it mid-project.

Who this is for

Data center operators, colocation providers, critical cooling contractors and facilities teams responsible for uptime reporting.

Honest limitations

  • No autonomous changes to live cooling, power or electrical systems. Ever, without engineering assessment, site procedures and written authorisation.
  • We do not replace DCIM. We read from what you have and add the analysis layer.
  • Sensor installation, network security certification and SLAs are out of scope unless separately agreed.
