Industrial service

Industrial AI Agents for Maintenance Teams

Agents built for technical teams: read the records, explain what changed, propose the next action, and wait for a person to approve it.

The operational problem

Maintenance teams are not short of data. They are short of time to read it. Work orders, alarms, service reports and spreadsheets each hold part of the picture, and nobody has an hour to assemble it before a shift handover.

What we build

  • Read-only ingestion of work orders, alarms and asset registers
  • Proposed actions with the evidence quoted, not a bare recommendation
  • Risk classes deciding what may run automatically, what needs a person, and what is never automatic
  • Hard caps per hour on any automated action, with escalation on breach
  • Full audit trail: what the agent knew, when, and what it proposed
  • Shift-handover summaries written from the actual records

Integration reality

We work with the data your site already produces: SNMP where the equipment exposes it, Modbus or OPC-UA where a gateway exists, vendor exports and BMS/DCIM reports where neither is available. Collection is read-only. If a protocol is not supported by your hardware, we say so before quoting rather than discovering it mid-project.

Who this is for

Industrial maintenance providers, facilities management companies and plant engineering teams moving from reporting to assisted decisions.

Honest limitations

  • We do not operate live plant. Control integration requires owner authorisation, qualified engineering review, validated fail-safe behaviour and testing outside production.
  • No savings percentages are promised before your own baseline is measured.
  • Data quality limits everything. If the records are wrong, the summaries will be wrong, and we will say so.
