Service

Business Process Automation

Connecting the tools you already pay for so work moves without copy and paste.

The problem

The cost is rarely one big process. It is the twenty small manual steps around it: copying from email into a sheet, into a ticket, into an invoice.

What we build

  • Workflow mapping before any build
  • Integration with your existing systems through their APIs
  • Error handling and retry instead of silent failure
  • A written record of every automated step

What it looks like in practice

We start with a read-only connection to the data you already produce, run it alongside your current process, and only add automated actions once the baseline is proven and the approval gate is agreed in writing.
