AI Chatbot Development

Chatbots for service intake, after-hours coverage and lead capture, connected to the systems you already use.

The problem

Most service businesses lose the after-hours and overflow calls that arrive when the office is closed. A chatbot that captures the details, classifies urgency and books the job recovers that work.

What we build

  • Website, WhatsApp and web widget deployment
  • Captures model, fault code, site access and contact before dispatch
  • Hands off to a human with a summary, never a raw transcript
  • Measured on captured jobs, not message volume

What it looks like in practice

We start with a read-only connection to the data you already produce, run it alongside your current process, and only add automated actions once the baseline is proven and the approval gate is agreed in writing.
