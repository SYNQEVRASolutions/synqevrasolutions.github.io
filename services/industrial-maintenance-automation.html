Industrial Maintenance Automation & Reporting

Turn maintenance records into decisions: scheduled work that actually gets tracked, vendor visits that are evidenced, and reports produced without an afternoon in Excel.

The operational problem

Compliance and preventive maintenance are usually tracked in whatever tool was available at the time. The result is a monthly scramble to prove work happened, and repeat failures nobody connected.

What we build

  • PM schedules with evidence capture per visit
  • Vendor and contractor visit records with photo and document attachments
  • Compliance reporting packs generated from records, not retyped
  • Repeat-failure detection per asset across sites
  • Asset registers that stay current as work is completed
  • Exportable reports for clients and auditors

Integration reality

We work with the data your site already produces: SNMP where the equipment exposes it, Modbus or OPC-UA where a gateway exists, vendor exports and BMS/DCIM reports where neither is available. Collection is read-only. If a protocol is not supported by your hardware, we say so before quoting rather than discovering it mid-project.

Who this is for

Facilities management companies, industrial maintenance contractors and multi-site property teams with reporting obligations.

Honest limitations

  • We do not certify compliance. Reports present your records; the judgement stays with your compliance lead.
  • Legacy records may need clean-up before automation, which is quoted separately.
  • No control of building systems.
