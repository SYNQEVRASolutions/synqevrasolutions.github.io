Industrial Compressor Monitoring & Analytics

Condition visibility for screw, reciprocating and centrifugal compressors, built on the sensors and controllers you already run.

The operational problem

Compressor failures are expensive in two directions: the repair, and the production stopped behind it. Most sites record run hours and faults, but rarely look at how temperature, current draw or vibration have moved against load condition.

What we build

  • Vibration, temperature, discharge pressure and motor current trends
  • Run-hour tracking per machine with service interval projection
  • Maintenance history attached to each asset
  • Anomaly review against the machine's own baseline, per load condition
  • Technician summaries: what changed, when, and what to inspect
  • An assistant that answers questions about a machine's recorded history

Integration reality

We work with the data your site already produces: SNMP where the equipment exposes it, Modbus or OPC-UA where a gateway exists, vendor exports and BMS/DCIM reports where neither is available. Collection is read-only. If a protocol is not supported by your hardware, we say so before quoting rather than discovering it mid-project.

Who this is for

Industrial maintenance companies, compressed air service providers and plants with a critical compressor house.

Honest limitations

  • We do not supply or install sensors. Where vibration data does not exist, the project starts with your instrumentation partner.
  • No guarantee of advance failure warnings. We present trend evidence and let your engineers judge.
  • No control of compressors, loading or unloading.
