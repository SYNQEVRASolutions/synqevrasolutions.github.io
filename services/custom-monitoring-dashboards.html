Industrial service

Custom Monitoring Dashboards & Industrial IoT Integration

When off-the-shelf dashboards do not match how your team actually works, we build the view around the decision, not around the data source.

The operational problem

Generic dashboards show every tag a device exposes. Operators need the four numbers that decide the next action, with the context that makes them meaningful, and a record of what changed since the last shift.

What we build

  • Read-only collection from Modbus, OPC-UA, SNMP and vendor APIs
  • Asset-first dashboards built around the decision, not the tag list
  • Alarm timelines and shift logs generated automatically
  • Scheduled reports delivered where your team already reads them
  • Historian-backed trend views with exportable evidence
  • Documented data model so another engineer can maintain it

Integration reality

We work with the data your site already produces: SNMP where the equipment exposes it, Modbus or OPC-UA where a gateway exists, vendor exports and BMS/DCIM reports where neither is available. Collection is read-only. If a protocol is not supported by your hardware, we say so before quoting rather than discovering it mid-project.

Who this is for

Plants, workshops and facilities with mixed equipment and no single vendor view, plus integrators who need a reporting layer for a client.

Honest limitations

  • No writes to PLCs, SCADA or control networks. Monitoring only, by default and by design.
  • Network segmentation and security review are the site owner's responsibility; we document what we need and nothing more.
  • Protocol support depends on your gateways and licences.
