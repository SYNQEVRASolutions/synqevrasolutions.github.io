Service

AI Voice Agents

Voice agents that answer, qualify and route calls, with a clean escalation to a person when the call needs one.

The problem

Phone coverage is the first thing that breaks under growth. A voice agent answers instantly, takes the details, and only escalates when a human decision is genuinely needed.

What we build

  • After-hours and overflow answering
  • Structured intake written into your job system
  • Escalation rules you define, not the model
  • Call recordings and transcripts retained for review

What it looks like in practice

We start with a read-only connection to the data you already produce, run it alongside your current process, and only add automated actions once the baseline is proven and the approval gate is agreed in writing.
