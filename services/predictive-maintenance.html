Service

Industrial Predictive Maintenance

Condition monitoring for compressors, pumps and cooling plant, focused on early warning rather than promised accuracy.

The problem

Failure rarely arrives without warning. Vibration, temperature and run-hour drift usually move first. The job is to surface the drift while a planned repair is still possible.

What we build

  • Baseline per machine and per load condition
  • Trend alerts with the evidence attached
  • Work-order creation with human approval
  • No claims of predicting failure without validated data

What it looks like in practice

We start with a read-only connection to the data you already produce, run it alongside your current process, and only add automated actions once the baseline is proven and the approval gate is agreed in writing.
