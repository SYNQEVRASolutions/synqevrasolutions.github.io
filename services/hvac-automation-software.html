Industrial service

HVAC Automation & Service Management Software

Less admin per job: intake, triage, scheduling and customer updates handled by software that writes into the tools your office already uses.

The operational problem

A service business loses margin in the gaps: the after-hours call that goes to voicemail, the repeat visit because the fault code was not captured, the maintenance contract that lapsed because nobody tracked it.

What we build

  • Service request intake across phone, web and messaging with structured fault capture
  • Urgency classification and dispatch summaries for the office
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling and renewal tracking
  • Technician knowledge assistant over your own manuals and past jobs
  • Customer status updates without a human typing them
  • Weekly operations reporting on volume, response and repeat visits

Integration reality

We work with the data your site already produces: SNMP where the equipment exposes it, Modbus or OPC-UA where a gateway exists, vendor exports and BMS/DCIM reports where neither is available. Collection is read-only. If a protocol is not supported by your hardware, we say so before quoting rather than discovering it mid-project.

Who this is for

HVAC and refrigeration contractors, mechanical service companies and facilities maintenance providers with a dispatch desk.

Honest limitations

  • We do not replace your field service software. We integrate with it through its API, or write to it where no API exists.
  • No guaranteed response-time or revenue outcomes.
  • Voice deployments depend on your telephony and on local call-recording rules.
