Data Center AI Monitoring

Monitoring and reporting for UPS, generator, cooling and power telemetry, with alarms compressed into decisions.

The problem

A site can produce hundreds of alarms a day. The useful signal is which three of them matter now, and what changed in the last hour.

What we build

  • Reads existing BMS, UPS and PDU exports
  • Alarm de-duplication and correlation
  • Shift and incident reports generated automatically
  • Illustrative dashboards only until connected to your real data

What it looks like in practice

We start with a read-only connection to the data you already produce, run it alongside your current process, and only add automated actions once the baseline is proven and the approval gate is agreed in writing.
