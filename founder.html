Biography

Mr. Fatir Ali is the Founder and Owner of SYNQEVRA Solutions, an AI-focused technology business developing intelligent agents, custom software, workflow automation, and practical digital solutions for businesses. His work focuses on exploring how artificial intelligence can simplify operations, improve information flow, and create new opportunities for businesses.

What he works on

  • Designing AI agents that operate against real telemetry, tickets and business records
  • Building approval gates, caps and audit trails so automation stays accountable
  • Writing the bridge between industrial data (UPS, PLC, cooling, compressors) and modern software
  • Keeping delivery honest: written scope, tested code, clearly labelled demonstrations

Working directly with the founder

Engagements are handled personally. Requirements, scope and delivery are agreed in writing before work begins, and the person who designs the system is the person who answers your questions about it.
