How an engagement works

  • 1. Read only. We connect to the data you already have and learn the real baseline for two weeks. No actions.
  • 2. Propose. The agent drafts the action with its evidence. A named person approves or rejects it.
  • 3. Gate. Risk classes decide what runs automatically, what needs a human, and what is never automatic.
  • 4. Cap. Actions per hour, spend and blast radius are capped. Hitting a cap escalates instead of improvising.
  • 5. Audit. Every decision is logged: what it knew, when, and what it proposed.
